Ballpark rules

During the at-bat: 15 = 2, 31 = 2, pair = 2, three alike = 6, four alike = 12, runs = their length. Go/last card = 1 unless it makes 31.

During the box score: score fifteens, pairs, runs, flush and his nobs. Non-dealer first, then dealer, then crib.

How to play

Objective. First to reach or pass 121 points wins immediately. If the losing player has fewer than 91, it's a skunk.

First deal. Both players cut; the lower card deals first (ties recut). The deal alternates each hand, beginning with the non-dealer. Each player receives six cards, discards two into the dealer's four-card crib, and the non-dealer cuts a starter. A starter Jack scores two for the dealer (“his heels”). The visual baseball theme does not change the rules of play.

Pegging. The non-dealer leads. Players alternate one card at a time; A = 1, faces = 10. The total never exceeds 31. Score 15 = 2, 31 = 2, pairs = 2/6/12 for two/three/four alike in succession, and runs of 3+ in any order. If you cannot legally play, say Go. Your opponent plays any cards that fit, taking 1 for the last card of the sequence (or 2 for 31, not both). Restart from zero, led by the player who did not play last if they have cards. The very last card of all pegging also scores 1 unless it made 31.

Counting. After all eight pegging cards, count the non-dealer's four cards plus the starter, then dealer's, then dealer's crib. Fifteens score 2 each; pairs 2 each; longest runs score their total length including duplicates; a flush is 4 for all four hand cards of one suit, or 5 with matching starter. A crib flush counts only when all five cards match. A Jack in the hand or crib matching the starter's suit earns 1 (“his nobs”).

Online scoring. The server deals, checks legal moves, and calculates every point automatically. Manual muggins claims are not part of this assisted-scoring version.
